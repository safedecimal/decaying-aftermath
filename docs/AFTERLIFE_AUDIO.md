Imported from the official MP3 URLs in `aftermath_audio_manifest.json`; converted to stereo 44.1 kHz Ogg Vorbis, no rearrangement.

The severe-low-mood track is the user-supplied `Numb (2).mp3` from Downloads. The user requested bundling this recording. It is rendered at 0.72 speed with lowered pitch, filtering, mild bit crushing and echo; source/rendered SHA-256 and the exact filter are in `aftermath_audio_manifest.json`. It is not a separate resource pack.

Rajdhani Light by Indian Type Foundry is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the original OFL.txt bytes are bundled as font/rajdhani_ofl.txt next to the unmodified font.

The mood test is strictly below -75 at the death event, regardless of damage type. The corpse and nearby players are anonymized by soft opaque monochrome smears; ordinary death has no such masks.
